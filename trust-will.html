01 / Flagship marketing project

Young Adult Plan: from audience insight to launch

I led GTM strategy for the Young Adult Plan, developing personas, positioning, messaging, channel strategy, and campaign ideas for college students, young professionals, and parents. The proposed campaigns were adopted and launched.

The positioning challenge was making estate planning feel relevant to people who don’t usually think about it yet. Each audience needed a different reason to care.

Young Adult Plan audiences, motivations, and messaging approach
AudienceWhat matters to themMessaging approach
College students18–21Freedom, safety, identity, and feeling in controlMade protection relevant to campus life without scare tactics
Young professionals22–26Security, simplicity, and growing independenceLinked legal planning to a first job, new benefits, and early financial goals
Prepared parentsBuying and influence audienceAn additional audience who can buy for, or influence, a young adultSpecific motivations and creative to be added once approved for public use
02 / Lifecycle marketing

Different customers need different next steps.

Registered, not paid

Personalized messages to clarify the value and support purchase completion.

Paid, unfinished

Reminders and guidance to help customers finish their documents.

Paid and finished

Onboarding, education, and relevant next-step or upgrade messaging.

I led development of the tailored email journey. Flows launched in Iterable, but I did not have access to conversion and engagement results, so no uplift is claimed.
